My Homestead HQ

Livestock records for small farms and homesteads.

Keep track of the things that actually matter when you're the one doing chores: who's bred, who's due, what treatment they had and when it clears, and what the whole thing is costing you.

  • Health records, vaccinations, and withdrawal periods
  • Breeding records, due dates, and multi-generation lineage
  • Costs and profit tracked per animal, not just per farm
  • Chores and reminders that don't nag you for missing a week
  • Cattle, goats, poultry — or set up your own animal in about thirty seconds
Works in the barn. Everything lives on your own device, so it works with no signal at all. No account to create, and nothing gets uploaded anywhere.
