Privacy Policy

My Homestead HQ · Effective [DATE]

The short version: My Homestead HQ does not collect your data. Your records stay on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting. We do not know who uses this app, how often, or what is in your records.

Where your records live

Everything you enter — animals, health records, breeding records, tasks, costs, and photos — is stored in a database on your own device. It is not uploaded anywhere. If your device is lost or damaged and you have no backup, those records are gone, because there is no copy on our end to restore from.

Device backups

If you have iCloud Backup or Google's device backup switched on, your phone may include this app's data in those backups. That is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, not by us. We have no access to those backups.

Photos and permissions

If you attach a photo to a record, the app asks for access to your camera or photo library at that moment. The photo is saved on your device alongside your records. It is not uploaded.

Exporting your data

You can export your records at any time. Where the exported file goes and who you share it with is entirely up to you — once it leaves the app, it is outside our control and outside this policy.

Third parties

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data brokers, and it does not share information with anyone, because it does not collect any.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Younger family members are welcome to use it to keep records — nothing they enter is collected either.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its records from your device. The app also includes a reset option if you want to clear your records without uninstalling. We cannot delete your data for you, because we never had it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting anything — for example if optional cloud backup or crash reporting is added in future — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the change will be explained in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@myhomesteadhq.com
